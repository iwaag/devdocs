# Workflow editor p8/ex1 — step 3: Simple graphs and contextual editing

**Source**: agdevworld `1d27a2f`. **Deployment**: none. The installed release is still `8cda5c9`.

## Cards

Simple has its own card. Compact and Mini are not variants of it.

- **What the card shows**:
  - a 34 px icon on a light tile (the node's appearance icon when it names one, else its type's);
  - the name (17 px, two lines at most);
  - in a run, a short state word with its glyph;
  - the report icon in the card's corner.
- **What it leaves out**: the type label, the description, the repository badges and, in a run, the long wait label.
  - The type stays in the card's accessible name ("Do node Verify") and its tooltip.
  - The full state label (for example "Waiting · Sample Person", or "Answered · Sample Agent to take up") stays in the pill's title.
  - "Answered" stays distinct from "Waiting" on the card itself.
  - A delegate keeps its target line.
- **Generated steps**: the plan talk, the report icons and the final report remain. In Simple the plan card has no description and the final report no file name. The report icon keeps its accessible label ("Report on Verify: report-verify.md").
- **Geometry** (`canvas.ts`, `SIMPLE`): 200 × 96, on the stored positions × 0.76. Positions are never rewritten. The graph opens at up to 90 % when it fits, otherwise at 90 % from its start, or from the run's current position. Fit shows the whole graph.
  - **Large pipeline** (16 nodes, 1440 × 1000): 14 of 18 cards are readable on opening. Before, p8's Compact view showed fewer at 72 %.
  - **Completed run**: 6 of 8 cards (step 2).

## Switching with a graph on screen

- **Same view.** The canvas is told the mode (`setSimple`) and redrawn by the view. The point of the graph at the canvas's centre stays at the centre, at the same zoom. Nothing is refit, whether Detail was Compact or Mini, whose position scales differ.
- **Selection.** The selection is kept. A selected card that the new card sizes or the side pane would hide is brought into view (`reveal`), with the pan only. The run view does the same when selecting a step makes the Simple side pane appear.
- **Compact/Mini.** The setting is offered only in Detail. It keeps its own key (`workflow-editor.mode`), so returning to Detail shows the person's previous choice.
- **Alignment.** Report icons, edges, dragging, connecting and hit targets all use the active metrics. After a switch, a drag or a connection, every edge starts at its source card's right edge and ends at its target's left edge, and every report icon sits inside its card.

## Selection in Simple

- **Definition, node.** Shows the icon and name, then the existing Name and Description fields (the same elements as Detail: one draft), its issues, and **More details**. More details switches to Detail, where the same inspector shows type, delegate target, appearance, repositories, connections, provenance and Delete. The keyboard lands on the inspector's heading.
- **Definition, connection.** Shows "Connection", its two ends as links, what waits for what, and More details. Delete is Detail's, and Delete/Backspace on the canvas still works.
- **Definition, generated step.** Shows its title, one line ("Generated by the documentation policy; it follows its card"), its purpose and More details. Its waits, its file name and the instructions given to the agent are Detail's. It never becomes an authored node.
- **Run, node or step.** Shows the name, state, description, recorded outcome, wait or failure, and its report with "Read it" (opens in the side pane's file card). A step at work also shows its latest observation. More details shows the full record (times, notes, its questions, template, revision) in Detail.
